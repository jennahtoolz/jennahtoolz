SurveySide for Chrome

1. Extract the ZIP to a folder you will keep.
2. In Chrome's address bar, open chrome://extensions
3. Turn Developer mode ON (top right).
4. Click Load unpacked. Choose the folder containing manifest.json.
5. Pin SurveySide from Chrome's puzzle-piece extensions menu.
6. Open your survey and click the SurveySide icon.
7. In Setup, add your Gemini API key and click Save setup.
   Get your own key at https://aistudio.google.com/apikey
8. Click Scan current page. Review the captured question.
9. Click Explain & circle best match to ask Gemini for help.
   A supported matching option gets an orange circle on the survey.
   Review it and select the answer yourself. Hide circle removes it.

For screen reading, check Include a screenshot of the visible tab
before scanning. The screenshot appears as a preview before sending.
Use Allow embedded survey sites if a survey frame needs permission.

No Python, npm, or Cloudflare setup is needed. Chrome 116+ is required.
The key lasts for this browser session; enter it again after restarting.
The extension explains and circles choices using your facts.
If facts are missing or the question changes, it does not guess a circle.
You choose the answer. Dropdown circles name the option without selecting it.
Provider qualification and screen-outs cannot be guaranteed.

Updating an existing installation: replace the extension files in the
same folder, then click Reload on its card at chrome://extensions.
Refresh the survey tab and re-enter the session key in Setup.

See README.md for data handling, permissions and testing limitations.
